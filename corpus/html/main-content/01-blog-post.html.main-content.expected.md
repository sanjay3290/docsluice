# Counting anemones at low tide

By the survey team, 14 May 2026

We counted green anemones along three transects at the north inlet during the lowest tide of the month.

Cover was highest on the sheltered side of the boulder field, where the water stays in shallow pools for hours.

## Method

Each transect was walked twice, and every anemone wider than two centimetres was recorded with its pool number.
