Harbour reopens after the winter dredging works

The inner harbour reopened to small boats on Monday after eleven weeks of dredging along the old quay.

Harbour staff said the channel is now two metres deeper at low water, which should end the groundings seen last summer.

Moorings will be reallocated in June, and owners are asked to confirm their berths by the end of May.
