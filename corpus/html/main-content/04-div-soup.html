Rules for plot holders

Plots must be cultivated from March to October, and at least three quarters of each plot must be in use.

Bonfires are allowed only on the first Saturday of the month, before ten in the morning and never in dry weather.

Water butts are shared between neighbouring plots, so please close the taps after filling your cans.
