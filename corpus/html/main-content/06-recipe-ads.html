Barley and leek soup

  • 200 grams of pearl barley, rinsed until the water runs clear
  • Two large leeks, washed and sliced into thin rounds

Soften the leeks in butter for ten minutes, then add the barley and two litres of vegetable stock.

Simmer for forty minutes, season with pepper, and finish with a handful of chopped parsley.
