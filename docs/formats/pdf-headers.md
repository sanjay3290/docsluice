# PDF repeated headers and footers

The private repeated-line helper classifies lines in the top and bottom 10% of each rotated page. It compares NFC-normalized text with whitespace runs collapsed and decimal digit runs replaced by `#`, then compares rounded page-relative line centers. A candidate group must occur on at least three distinct pages and on at least 60% of the document. If it does not meet the document-wide threshold, an alternating odd/even header variant can qualify when it independently occurs on at least three pages and at least 60% of that parity cohort. Footer groups always require the document-wide threshold.

With `pdfHeaders: "remove"` (the issue-authorized default), classified occurrences are removed from their pages and each group is returned once with its role, representative text and line, position, and source page indices. `pdfHeaders: "keep"` preserves every page line and returns no header/footer groups. Text repeated in the body is never a candidate. Before normalizing a candidate, the helper preflights the candidate source length plus unique normalized text already staged against the shared output allowance. It does not charge that allowance; the caller's `DocBuilder` must charge emitted blocks once. If preflight truncates, or the shared budget is already truncated or the child-depth view is unavailable, the helper preserves all input lines and emits no groups. The #46 layout stage remains responsible for bounding its original line buffers.

This helper is private preparation code. It does not parse PDFs, choose an engine, or register public options. It assumes page-relative `LayoutLine` geometry from #46 and still needs the #45 text-item adapter and #27 corpus. Synthetic unit tests validate the helper's classification rules only; real-PDF extraction accuracy, end-to-end output goldens, cross-runtime behavior, and threshold calibration remain unverified.
