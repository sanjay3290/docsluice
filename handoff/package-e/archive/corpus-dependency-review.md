# Package E image/archive corpus and candidate-output review

Read-only review of the prepared fixtures and the Package E integration worktrees. No repository source, shared manifest, or fixture bytes were changed.

## Fixture facts

- The image generator produces 11 original synthetic files: a complete 1×1 RGB PNG and 1×1 GIF89a; little- and big-endian TIFF metadata snippets with dimensions 3×2, orientation 6, `SyntheticCo` / `Unit Camera`, capture time `2019:04:05 12:34:56`, and GPS DMS `N 1°2′3″`, `W 4°5′6″`; a JPEG SOI/APP1 Exif/SOF0 snippet with dimensions 5×3 and the same time but no SOS scan; three WebP VP8/VP8L/VP8X+Exif structural snippets with header dimensions 5×3; and three malformed TIFF samples (cyclic IFD, count 65,535 without entries, and an out-of-file value extent). Every one of the 11 has a CC0-1.0 sidecar. The prepared source facts and all 22 binary/sidecar SHA-256 values match `images/expected-metadata.json`.
- The archive generator produces 15 original synthetic ZIP/GZIP/TAR binaries (total 1,166,070 bytes; largest 1,060,022 bytes), each below its 15 MiB cap, with 15 CC0-1.0 sidecars. ZIP content covers ordered CSV/HTML/nested ZIP/PNG/directory entries, a path-traversal name plus OS junk, exactly 10,000 empty entries, and four-level nesting around a bounded 512 KiB leaf. GZIP covers CSV, two concatenated members, FEXTRA/FNAME/FCOMMENT/FHCRC, and bounded 512 KiB expansion. TAR covers nested ustar files/directories/symlink metadata, PAX and GNU long names, checksum/size/PAX-size malformed structures, plus gzip-wrapped TAR. The stdlib validators independently decode valid members and assert intended malformed bytes; they never extract member paths to disk.
- In the Package E integration worktree, all 11 image binaries and their CC0 sidecars are present under `corpus/images/` with bytes identical to the prepared originals. All 11 GZIP/TAR binaries and sidecars are present under `corpus/tar/` byte-for-byte. `corpus/zip/package-e-mixed.zip` is byte-identical to prepared `zip-mixed.zip`. The three additional ZIP security/stress candidates (`zip-hostile-names.zip`, `zip-10000-empty.zip`, `zip-nested-amplification.zip`) remain preparation-only and are not in that corpus tree.
